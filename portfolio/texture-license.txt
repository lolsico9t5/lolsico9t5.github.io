The surface maps and Saturn ring texture in surfaces.webp are adapted from
Solar System Scope (INOVE): https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Maps were resized, combined into a WebP atlas, and rendered with animated
longitude, illumination, atmospheric shading, and rings. Earth combines the
day and cloud maps as separately rotating layers. Saturn's rings cast a
directional shadow on the globe. The gas and ice giants include illustrative weather
features for visibility at small sizes. Ceres uses the collection's
illustrative Ceres map.
